About Us

Taking academic theory and applying it to real life

A hybrid institution of higher learning with the mandate to train, examine and certify entrepreneurs and artisans for proficiency and cognitive manpower development.

Uyo Business School has acquired all the necessary certifications that safeguard its existence and operations as a legitimate educational institution, with business premises in Uyo, the Akwa Ibom State capital. It is currently an accredited body for the training and examination of Computer Marshal candidates for the award of the CMA+ certification.

In partnership with a federal-government-licensed Recruiter Agency, the school gained approval through the Ministry of Education to train artisans and entrepreneurs across various trades and convert their skills into Competency Based Education Certification (COBEC) — forming a Business Data Bank of competent professionals available to industries and government parastatals in need of a capable labour force.

We mold and craft people into astute decision-makers, business professionals and top-notch managers who can readily navigate business uncertainties. To this end, the school designed the globally recognised Master of Business Advancement (MBA+) — a registered trademark of Uyo Business School through the Federal Ministry of Trade and Industry.
